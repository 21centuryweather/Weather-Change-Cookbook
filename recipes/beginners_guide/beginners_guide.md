# A true beginner's guide to climate and atmospheric science coding

Hi! Welcome to climate and atmospheric science, we are thrilled to have you join us. This is a series of recipes written for people with zero coding experience. The goal is to help you gain some confidence, learn the basics, and arm yourself with the knowledge to start navigating coding closer to your original purpose, your research question. 

Each section it's made build from a Jupyter notebook, that independently of the other sections, and you will see a lot of repetition, this is to help you learn skills. At the end of each notebook there are exercises for you to practice, some are code with error errors you need to fix, other have missing lines of code. These were designed for you to have a go at learning how to develop skills on your own and read and diagnose common error messages. You will also fin de answers hidden at the end. You can use them to check your answers. If you are stuck with an exercise, there is NO shame in looking at the answers script to help you fill out the blanks, this is a valid learning strategy that I implore you to use. 

We use a few examples here, including solar data and synoptic weather types. We recommend running everything through with the data we have provided for simplicity while learning, and once you feel confident, have a go using some data that is more relevant to your project. 

Best of luck with your coding journey, We know it can be stressful and overwhelming learning a new skill, but We promise you’ll pick it up in no time! And remember, there are plenty of people who can help, we were all beginners once!
